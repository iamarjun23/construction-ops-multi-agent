ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF WORK

1.1 The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and
supervision necessary to complete the construction work described in the
Project Schedule for Riverside Tower, organized by zone (Zone 1 through
Zone 4) and by milestone within each zone.

1.2 Each zone's milestones follow the standard sequence: Sitework &
Excavation, Framing, Drywall, Electrical Rough-in, Plumbing Rough-in, and
Final Finish. Milestones must be completed in sequence within a zone unless
the Client's project manager approves an exception in writing.

ARTICLE 2 — CONTRACT SUM AND SCHEDULE OF VALUES

2.1 The Client shall pay the Contractor the amounts set forth in the
Schedule of Values for each milestone, subject to the conditions of this
Article and Article 4.

2.2 Payment for a milestone becomes due within fifteen (15) business days
of the milestone being marked complete, subject to the verification
requirements in Article 4.

ARTICLE 3 — MILESTONE COMPLETION

3.1 A milestone is considered complete when the Contractor's site
supervisor certifies in the daily progress log that all work items for
that milestone have been finished to the specifications in the Project
Schedule.

3.2 Completion under Section 3.1 establishes the Contractor's entitlement
to payment under Article 2, subject to Article 4's verification
conditions. Completion alone does not waive the Client's rights under
Article 4.

ARTICLE 4 — INSPECTION AND VERIFICATION

4.1 General. The Client's project manager or designated representative may
inspect completed work at any time. The Contractor shall provide
reasonable access to the work site for this purpose.

4.2 Inspection Photograph Requirement. For any milestone involving
work that will be concealed by a subsequent milestone (including, without
limitation, Drywall, which conceals Electrical Rough-in and Plumbing
Rough-in), the Contractor shall submit date-stamped inspection
photographs documenting the completed work to the Client's project
manager before payment for that milestone is released. The Client may
withhold payment for the affected milestone until such photographs are
received and accepted. Withholding under this Section 4.2 shall not
exceed thirty (30) calendar days from the date the milestone was marked
complete under Section 3.1; if the Contractor has not cured the
deficiency within that period, the matter shall be referred to the
dispute resolution procedure in Article 5.

4.3 Rejection. If inspection photographs submitted under Section 4.2
reveal non-conforming work, the Client's project manager shall notify the
Contractor in writing within five (5) business days, specifying the
deficiency. The milestone shall not be considered complete under Section
3.1 until the deficiency is cured.

ARTICLE 5 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

5.1 Any dispute arising from withheld payment, rejected inspection, or
disagreement over milestone completion shall first be addressed through a
joint meeting of the Client's and Contractor's project managers within ten
(10) business days of either party's written request.

5.2 If the dispute is not resolved under Section 5.1, either party may
pursue mediation before initiating any other proceeding. Nothing in this
Article shall be construed as legal advice or a determination of legal
validity; it describes the parties' contractual process only.

ARTICLE 6 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 This Contract, together with the Project Schedule and the Inspection
& Quality Assurance Policy referenced herein, constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties regarding the Riverside Tower project.

6.2 Amendments to this Contract must be in writing and signed by both
parties' authorized representatives.

6.3 Retainage of five percent (5%) may be withheld from each milestone
payment per the Company-Wide Payment Terms & Retainage Policy, in addition
to any withholding under Section 4.2.
