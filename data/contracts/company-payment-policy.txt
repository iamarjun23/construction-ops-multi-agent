COMPANY-WIDE PAYMENT TERMS & RETAINAGE POLICY
Applies to all active projects unless superseded by a project-specific
Master Construction Contract.

This dataset is synthetic and generated to reproduce realistic cross-system
retrieval challenges. It is not derived from any real client engagement.

1. RETAINAGE

1.1 A retainage of five percent (5%) is withheld from every milestone
payment across all projects and released upon Final Finish milestone
acceptance for the applicable zone.

2. PAYMENT METHODS

2.1 Payments are made by ACH transfer or company check, at the Client's
discretion, within the timeframe specified in the applicable project's
Master Construction Contract.

3. DISPUTED OR WITHHELD PAYMENTS

3.1 Where a project-specific contract permits withholding payment pending
inspection verification (see each project's Article 4), that provision
controls over this policy. This policy does not independently authorize
withholding beyond what the project contract states.

3.2 Any amount withheld must be tracked against the specific milestone it
relates to; withholding on one milestone does not affect payment due on
other, unrelated milestones in the same zone or project.

4. LATE PAYMENT

4.1 Amounts not disputed and not subject to a withholding provision that
remain unpaid more than thirty (30) calendar days past the due date accrue
interest at the rate specified in the applicable Master Construction
Contract, or 1% per month if none is specified.
