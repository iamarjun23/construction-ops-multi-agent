ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF WORK

1.1 The Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and
supervision necessary to complete the construction work described in the
Project Schedule for Harbor Point Logistics Center, organized by zone
(Zone A through Zone C) and by milestone within each zone.

1.2 Each zone's milestones follow the standard sequence: Sitework &
Excavation, Framing, Drywall, Electrical Rough-in, Plumbing Rough-in, and
Final Finish.

ARTICLE 2 — CONTRACT SUM AND SCHEDULE OF VALUES

2.1 The Client shall pay the Contractor the amounts set forth in the
Schedule of Values for each milestone, subject to the conditions of this
Article and Article 4.

2.2 Payment for a milestone becomes due within twenty (20) business days
of the milestone being marked complete, subject to Article 4.

ARTICLE 3 — MILESTONE COMPLETION

3.1 A milestone is considered complete when the Contractor's site
supervisor certifies in the daily progress log that all work items for
that milestone have been finished to the specifications in the Project
Schedule.

ARTICLE 4 — INSPECTION AND VERIFICATION

4.1 General. The Client's project manager may inspect completed work at
any time with reasonable notice.

4.2 Inspection Photograph Requirement. For concealment milestones
(Drywall, and any milestone that covers prior electrical or plumbing
work), the Contractor shall submit date-stamped inspection photographs to
the Client's project manager before payment is released. The Client may
withhold payment until such photographs are received, for a period not to
exceed forty-five (45) calendar days from milestone completion.

ARTICLE 5 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

5.1 Disputes over withheld payment or milestone completion shall first be
addressed through a joint meeting of the parties' project managers within
ten (10) business days of either party's written request.

ARTICLE 6 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 This Contract, together with the Project Schedule and the Company-Wide
Payment Terms & Retainage Policy, constitutes the entire agreement between
the parties regarding the Harbor Point Logistics Center project.

6.2 Retainage of five percent (5%) may be withheld from each milestone
payment per the Company-Wide Payment Terms & Retainage Policy.
