RIVERSIDE TOWER — INSPECTION & QUALITY ASSURANCE POLICY
Referenced by Article 6.1 of the Master Construction Contract.

This dataset is synthetic and generated to reproduce realistic cross-system
retrieval challenges. It is not derived from any real client engagement.

1. PURPOSE

This policy sets the operational procedure for the inspection photograph
requirement in Article 4.2 of the Master Construction Contract. It does
not create obligations beyond those in the Contract; where the two
conflict, the Contract controls.

2. PHOTOGRAPH SUBMISSION PROCEDURE

2.1 Photographs must be submitted through the project's shared site log
within five (5) business days of the milestone being marked complete.

2.2 Each submission must include: (a) a date stamp, (b) the zone and
milestone identifier, and (c) sufficient coverage to verify the concealed
work meets the Project Schedule specification.

2.3 The project manager reviews submissions within three (3) business days
of receipt and records the outcome (accepted or rejected) in the
milestone's record.

3. CONSEQUENCES OF MISSING PHOTOGRAPHS

3.1 A milestone marked complete without accepted inspection photographs
remains in "payment withheld" status per Article 4.2 of the Contract. The
project's payment record will show the milestone as complete but unpaid.

3.2 Site supervisors are responsible for flagging missing photographs in
the daily progress log so the omission is visible to both the Contractor
and the Client's project manager.

4. ESCALATION

4.1 If photographs remain outstanding for more than twenty (20) calendar
days after milestone completion, the project manager shall send written
notice to the Contractor referencing the thirty (30) day limit in Article
4.2 of the Contract.

4.2 This policy does not authorize indefinite withholding; the Article 4.2
thirty-day limit governs.
